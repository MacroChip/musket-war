      How to fight

      • WASD move · mouse aim · click fire · R reload (follow the steps!)
      • Stay near your neighbors — if half your line's tethers snap, your army routs.
      • Musician: hit arrow keys in time; each hit plays a whole phrase of the tune, and good music tightens your team's aim.
      • Medic: press E by a downed man: whiskey, then leeches.
      • Retreat: routed men flee (press F to drop bear traps, 2 each); pursuers fix bayonets and charge at the whistle.
      • Short on friends? Muster AI soldiers 🤖 onto either team — they hold the line, volley, and revive. Dismiss one with ✕, or click ⚖ to even the sides.
